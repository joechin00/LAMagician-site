About

The Jack of Hearts

Joseph Kerr — known to his audiences as @MagicalKnights — has spent over a decade making rooms full of strangers lean in at once.

An 11-year member of Hollywood’s Magic Castle, Joseph is a 7-time award winner at its Strolling Magic Competition — including two first-place victories, tied for the most awards in the competition’s twenty-year history. His material is thoughtful and original: simple premises, taken somewhere no one expects.
